# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org)
v2.1. In short: we are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of background or identity.

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include showing
empathy and kindness, respecting differing opinions, giving and gracefully
accepting constructive feedback, and focusing on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, trolling, insulting or derogatory
comments, and publishing others' private information without permission.

Instances of abusive or unacceptable behavior may be reported to the maintainers
at chester.ismay@gmail.com. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated
promptly and fairly.

The full text of the Contributor Covenant is available at
<https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>.
